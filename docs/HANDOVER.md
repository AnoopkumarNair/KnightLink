## Full scan, discovery polling and phone sensors (2026-11-17, uncommitted, passes the full check)

For the next car trip, which is the only one before more work: a parked, one-shot, read-only **Full scan** from the connection card, then a drive during which the identifiers the scan found are polled raw, with the phone's motion sensors and GPS recorded beside the traffic. Everything is in the recording (schema 3 when sensors are on) and the connection log; read it back with `tools/cli`.

- **Where it lives.** New module `core/scan` (`FullScan`, `DidSweepPlan`, `DtcDecoder`, `MonitorStatus`/`Mode06Decoder`, `ScanResult`); the data file `definitions/discovery/did-sweep.json` (ranges, candidates per control unit, pace; validated by `DidSweepPlan` and `DidSweepFileTest`); `VehicleSession.startScan()/cancelScan()/dismissScan()` and `scan: StateFlow<ScanState>`; `discoveryPollItems` (runtime); `PollScheduler.replaceExtras`; `ExternalSamples` and `EventKind.EXTERNAL` (recorder); `TrafficExchanges` (recorder, for analysis); in the app `PhoneSamples.kt`/`AndroidSampleSource.kt`, the scan section of the connection card, the "Drive data" card (location permission); in the CLI `fullscan-report` and `did-timeline`.
- **Stages** (each written as `scan` events plus the raw traffic): 1 standard probe (names, calibration ids, masked VIN); 2 readiness (`0101`, `0141`, Mode 06 bitmaps and every supported monitor id); 3 discovery of control units over 0x700-0x7F7 (not 0x7DF) with `22 F190` (and `22 F187` when F190 is refused), physical, one request id at a time, with the adapter listening to every 7xx identifier (`ATCF700`, `ATCM700`); 4 identification `22 F180-F1FF` of every control unit found; 5 fault codes (`03`, `07`, `0A` on the OBD units, `19 02 FF` on all) sorted by status (confirmed, pending, warning lamp, failing a test; codes whose test has not run are counted apart and are never listed as faults); 6 sweep of the identifiers in the plan on 7E1, 7E0, 7E4 (candidates first). The result is one JSON line (`scan-result` event, VIN masked) and, in the app, `<recording>.scan.json` beside the recording.
- **Safety.** Only services `ReadOnlyPolicy` already allowed. The policy gained exactly `ATCF`, `ATCM` (3 or 8 hex digits), `ATFCSH` (3 or 8), `ATFCSD` (data starting `30`, i.e. ClearToSend) and `ATFCSM` (0 or 1), with tests. The scan reads PID 0D on 7E0 before it starts, every 40 requests and at least every 2 s; at speed above 0 it puts the adapter back to its normal settings and the session polls normally for 5 s at a time until the speed reads 0 again; a speed that cannot be read refuses the scan (at the start) or stops it. At most 20 requests/s (the driver's 20 ms ceiling stays); battery voltage every 30 s. A control unit that answers anything but a value or "request out of range" or "security access denied" to a data identifier (conditions not correct, response pending, ...) is not asked further; one that is silent and does not answer a ping (something it answered before) stops the whole scan. A unit that ignores identifiers it does not have is left after 48 unanswered ones in a row (per range).
- **Long replies.** Sweeps ask with the adapter's "one frame is enough" digit, because without it every request costs the adapter's timeout. A reply that is cut to its first frame (a positive answer longer than one frame) is asked again, whole and without the digit, after 0.5 s so the control unit gives up the unfinished transfer. Control units with non-OBD identifiers get `ATFCSH/ATFCSD/ATFCSM1` set for them (as Car Scanner does); the OBD units use the adapter's automatic flow control. `ElmResponse.respondingIds` says which identifiers sent any frame, so a cut reply still proves the unit is there.
- **Discovery polling.** After the scan, every identifier answered on 7E1 and up to 20 on 7E0 and 7E4 with at most 8 data bytes (at most 60 in all; the limits are `pollLimit`/`pollMaxBytes` in the plan) are polled as raw service 22 items, one request each (never batched), all at one period (1 s for few, up to 8 s for many: about 8 requests/s together). They share the budget with the normal items by the scheduler's ordinary rule. They survive a reconnect (not an app restart). Identifiers are recorded as provenance `inferred`.
- **Phone sensors.** While a session runs the app records linear acceleration (the plain accelerometer, gravity included, when the phone has none) and rotation at 25 Hz, delivered in half-second batches (`mot` lines), and GPS speed, bearing, accuracy and position once a second (`gps` lines) when the user has allowed location (card "Drive data"); without it only motion is recorded. A `sensors` line says what the phone has. The raw recording keeps precise positions (app-private); the scrubbed share rounds latitude and longitude to 2 decimals (about 1 km) and a `gps` line that cannot be read loses its position (`ExternalSamples.scrub`, tested).
- **Scrubber.** It now also scrubs the VIN read as `22 F190`, and keeps a reply cut to its first frame (it holds only the manufacturer code). Recordings from before this change replay as before; schema 3 is written only when sensors are on (`RecordingTransport(externalSamples = true)`).
- **Demo.** The demo scenario is now `fixtures/sim/harrier-full-scan.json` (the DPF scenario plus body control units, identification data, manufacturer identifiers, fault codes, parked at speed 0); the full scan runs against it in about 10 minutes of real time (the simulated adapter takes 45 ms per command). `VirtualElm327` now models the receive filter (only 7E8-7EF until `ATCF`/`ATCM`) and flow control, `VirtualEcu` can refuse a request with a negative code, stay silent, or ignore identifiers it does not have.
- **CLI.** `fullscan-report <recording>` prints the result (control units and identities with VIN masked, readiness, fault codes by status, which identifiers answered per unit). `did-timeline <recording> <ecu> <did>` prints one identifier's raw bytes at each read next to the car's speed and rpm and the phone's acceleration and GPS speed.
- **Expected duration on the car:** about 9 minutes parked (8 to 12). Sweep: 6150 requests at the 20/s pace is 5.1 min, 5.7 with the speed checks and the second read of long answers; control unit discovery about 1 min (every absent identifier costs the adapter's 200 ms timeout); identification about 1.5 min; probe, readiness and fault codes about 30 s. A control unit that ignores identifiers it does not have costs up to 1.5 min more (the rest of a range is left after 48 unanswered). The brief's 5 minutes is not reachable at 20 requests/s; shorten the sweep by giving a target its own `ranges` in the plan.

## Body-unit check, parked stage and dashboard stamps (2026-11-23)

Replaces the one-time candidate check that went through the normal poll path (it never set the adapter's receive filter, so every reply of 700/701 was lost as NO DATA).

- **Where.** `BodyUnitReader` (`core/runtime`), `VehicleSession.runBodyStage/runBodySlice`, candidate file `definitions/discovery/candidates/<vehicle>.json` (`CandidateChecks`, limit 2048 identifiers; the Harrier file asks 700, 701, 703, 705, 750, all `unprobed`, F180-F1FF everywhere, 700 also 0100-01FF and DD00-DDFF).
- **Per body unit, in this order:** `ATCRA<id+8>` (fallback `ATCM7FF` + `ATCF<id+8>` if the adapter does not know `ATCRA`), `ATFCSH<id>`, `ATFCSD300000`, `ATFCSM1`, then `19 02 FF`, then `22 xxxx` for every identifier (count digit 1, a reply cut to its first frame is asked again, after 1 s, whole). `10 01` is not sent up front: it makes the unit start its default session afresh (which would end an extended session another tool opened), so it is sent once per unit only when the first plain `22` was answered with a refusal, and that identifier is asked again; the driver always sends `ATSH<unit>` right before it. Afterwards `ATAR`, `ATCM7F8`, `ATCF7E8`, `ATFCSM0` (and `driver.reinitialize` if the adapter refuses one). Restore happens in a `finally` (also on error and cancellation); a reconnect starts with a fresh init script anyway. Every one of these commands and the adapter's answer is a `body-setup` event; `body-scan` events say what each step answered (identifiers and lengths, never values; the raw frames are in the recording).
- **Parked stage.** After the probe and before polling: PID 0D on 7E0 is read; at speed 0 the units are asked at about 16 requests/s, the speed is read again every 100 requests / 5 s (the receive address is switched to 7E8 for that and back), for at most 120 s (`bodyStageCap`). Speed above 0, unreadable, the cap, or a lost link end it; the rest is asked in the background: every 15 s of polling, up to 15 requests (about 1 a second), with the same receive setup and restore. `VehicleSession.bodyScan` (`BodyScanState`) carries progress and seconds left; the app shows a banner and, when the parked stage ends, a heads-up notification and (if spoken status is on) a spoken line. NO DATA from a body unit never reaches the silence watchdog, the asleep logic or the bus-fault logic: those requests do not go through the poll loop. A unit with no reply to its first 12 requests is taken as absent.
- **Only on a known car.** The candidate file's `appliesTo` lists VIN prefixes (the Harrier file: `MAT`); the check runs only when the probed VIN starts with one, otherwise a `body-scan` event says `skipped: not a known vehicle` and nothing is sent. Background slices keep the scheduler's timing (`PollingLoop` has one clock per connection and the scheduler is not restarted after a slice) and are not started while nothing has answered for 2 s or every answer is NO DATA. A unit that only said "not now" (22, 21, 78 ...) is not finished: the attempt is counted and it is asked again from there, up to 5 times.
- **Once per vehicle.** Progress is kept in `VehicleMemory.body` (`BodyCheckMemory`, per request id: identifiers asked, replies, attempts, done), valid for the same candidate file id. A unit is done when it answered anything (also a refusal); one that never answered is tried again on later sessions, three attempts in all. Without a memory store (or before a vehicle key exists) it is once per app session.
- **Safety rule 10.** `ReadOnlyPolicy` now allows exactly `10 01` (default session) and only for a request id from 700 to 7FF outside 7DF-7EF (a body unit); every diagnostic request must go to 700-7FF (`requestIdViolation`, checked in `Elm327Driver.request`); `10 02`, `10 03`, `3E`, `27`, `2E`, `31`, `11`, `14` and everything else stay refused, also for body units (tests in `ReadOnlyGuardTest`). It also allows `ATCRA` with 3 or 8 hex digits or none. The driver passes the request id to the gate; a raw `command("1001")` is refused.
- **Dashboard stamps.** The Drive card has odometer and range (distance to empty) fields for the start and the end of a drive (`DashboardStore`, kept in SharedPreferences). They go into the recording as `odo <km> start|end` and `dte <km> start|end` external lines (`ExternalSamples`, scrubbed to a whole number and the word start/end) and into `app` events; the start values are written again into every new recording once polling starts. The battery voltage of the connection is a `battery` event (the probe's `ATRV`, or one read when the probe was reused).
- **Simulation.** `VirtualElm327` models `ATCRA`; `VirtualEcu.needsCustomFlowControl` makes a unit that is heard only with the receive address and flow control set. `BodyUnitSimulationTest` covers the stage, restore, moving car, cap, silent unit, `10 01` once, once per vehicle.

## Tyre sensor capture (2026-11-18)

Passive, receive-only capture of the tyre sensors' BLE advertisements during every session. **First version (BA06 manufacturer data) heard nothing in 44 minutes of driving on 2026-11-18**: the SensAiry app (com.tymtix.sensairy 2.10.07, read on the phone for interoperability) shows why. It uses a copy of the AltBeacon library and listens for two standard beacon formats: Eddystone-UID (service data under UUID FEAA, frame type 00) and iBeacon (Apple company id 004C, data starting 0215). It reads pressure as the first two bytes of the Eddystone instance (or the iBeacon major) shifted right by 5, in kPa (shown in psi after /6.89576 and an altitude correction of about 0.16 psi), and temperature as the next byte, signed, in degC; internal sensors on iBeacon send raw counts that the app converts with per-sensor calibration we do not have. It tells sensors apart by Bluetooth address. Also, the app used to declare `BLUETOOTH_SCAN` with `neverForLocation`, which makes Android filter beacon advertisements out of scan results: that flag is gone, so scanning now also needs the location permission (asked with the Bluetooth ones) and the phone's Location setting on.

How it works now: `BleAdvertisementListener` runs a balanced-mode scan with one hardware `ScanFilter` per layout in `tpmsDefinitions` (`app-config.json`): service data or manufacturer data, plus the data prefix (`definitions/tpms/sensairy.json` = Eddystone, `sensairy-ibeacon.json` = iBeacon). It never connects or writes and never hands out an address. Each advertisement becomes `tpms s:FEAA <data hex> <rssi>` (service data) or `tpms 004C <data hex> <rssi>` (manufacturer data), at most one per distinct payload per 5 s and 20 per second (`TyreSensorCapture`); the scrubber rewrites `tpms` lines from their parsed fields only. The "Drive data" card lists recent payloads read with the layout they fit, marked unconfirmed, with no alerts. `tools/cli tpms-report <recording>` lists each payload with its reading and, per sensor id guess (Eddystone namespace, iBeacon UUID), the share of moving minutes it was heard in (`car` / `spare?` / `other`). Not built yet: automatic learning of the car's own sensors and an allowlist.

## Vehicle safety guarantees (audited 2026-11-17)

- **Read-only, enforced in one place.** `ReadOnlyPolicy` (`core/adapter`) is checked in `Elm327Driver.exchangeOnce`, the only write to the adapter, before any byte leaves the phone. Diagnostic requests: only OBD 01/02/03/06/07/09/0A, UDS 22 and UDS 19 with a reporting sub-function. Also UDS 10 with sub-function 01 (default session) to a body unit only (request id outside 7DF-7EF). Refused: 04, 08, any other service 10, 11, 14, 27, 28, 2E, 2F, 31, 34–37, 3D, 3E, 85 and everything else. Adapter commands: an allowlist of volatile settings and queries, including the receive and flow-control settings `ATCF`, `ATCM`, `ATFCSH`, `ATFCSD` (ClearToSend data `30...` only), `ATFCSM` (0 or 1) and `ATCRA` (3 or 8 hex digits, or none), which put no message on the bus. `ATPP` (EEPROM), `AT@3`, `ATCV`, `ATWM`/`ATSW` (adapter-originated bus messages), `ATBI`, monitor modes, K-line inits, chipset (`ST`/`VT`) commands and a bare carriage return (repeats the previous command) are refused. Adapter definitions are validated against the same policy when they load. Tests: `ReadOnlyGuardTest`.
- **What the app actually sends today:** services 01 and 09 in normal use; the full scan, when asked for, also 03, 06, 07, 0A, 19 `02` and 22, and the `ATCF/ATCM/ATFC*` settings above. The probe sends `0100` to 7E0–7E7, support bitmaps, and 09 VIN/CALID/ECU name; polling sends service 01 PIDs from the definitions. Adapter commands: `ATD ATZ ATE0 ATL0 ATS0 ATH1 ATSP6 ATAT1 ATST32/64 ATSH ATI AT@1 ATRV ATDPN`. `ATSP6` means no multi-protocol search on the bus.
- **Bus load.** One request in flight at a time, each waiting for its reply or timeout. A hard ceiling of at least 20 ms between diagnostic requests (≤ 50/s; `Elm327Driver.Config.minRequestInterval`, cannot be set below 10 ms). The reference adapter measures about 22 requests/s, about 50–100 CAN frames/s with replies, a few percent of a 500 kbit/s bus, like any scan tool. Re-init and watchdog recovery only send adapter commands. "Waiting for ignition" re-probes at most every 4 s.

## Known open issues

- 29-bit CAN vehicles: the driver rejects them and the probe reports a misleading "every address failed". Planned for M5.
- "Response pending" (7F xx 78) is handled by the driver since 2026-11-19: it keeps reading the same request's replies (no resend) up to 3 more rounds or 5 s, and a final answer in the same read replaces the pending one. Untested against a car that really sends the final answer late; look for `response-pending` events.
- Driver timeouts are configurable now (adapter definition `timing`, `VehicleSession.Config.driver`); the values for slow BLE adapters still have to be found in the car.
- No overall time limit on the capability probe.
- Telemetry keeps last values while reconnecting; the rules engine (M3) must check reading age.
- Only OBD service 01 parameters are probed and polled; UDS (service 22) identifiers in a definition are ignored (the discovery items of a scan are polled raw, and not decoded).
- TPMS byte offsets in `definitions/tpms/sensairy.json` are marked `inferred`; they need a raw advertisement capture to confirm.
- The VIN scrubber finds the VIN in the service 09 reply and in `22 F190`; a VIN delivered any other way is not found.
- The full scan has not run on a car yet. Unknown until then: whether the adapter takes `ATCF/ATCM/ATFC*` (the scan says so in its warnings and goes on with the 7E8-7EF units), whether the body control units answer from request id + 8, how long absent identifiers take, and whether any control unit objects to a sweep (the scan then leaves it alone and says why).
- Demo mode runs as an ordinary (not foreground) service by design, so Android may stop it in the background.
- Bluetooth: no pairing/bonding handling; no location-toggle check on Android 11 and older.
- `VirtualElm327.received` grows without bound in a long demo.
- `fixtures/raw/` is still empty: the Phase 0 logs from the SRS were never supplied.

## Fixes after the 2026-11-18 drives (2026-11-19)

- **Remembered per vehicle** (`VehicleMemory`, a file per vehicle in app-private storage, no VIN: the key is a salted SHA-256 of VIN + calibration ids, the probe is stored redacted, the scan has masked VINs). On connect the last probe is reused when every ECU in it answers `01 00` with the same support bitmap (the only extra request); otherwise a full probe runs. The last scan and the discovery identifiers are restored, so discovery polling survives a new session. F4xx identifiers (they mirror Mode 01 PIDs) are labelled so in the scan result and not polled. Discovery items that never changed over 10 answers are asked once per session, the rest every 5 to 8 s; an item that changes after being "once" goes back to the slow period. Events: `memory`.
- **Scan**: one NRC 0x22 or 0x31 skips that identifier (five 0x22 in a row stop the unit); a lost link ends the scan as `LINK_LOST` with the partial result kept, shown as "link lost", and "Resume scan" carries on from the last completed stage (also restored after an app restart). "Run again" asks first. `fullscan-report` prefers the last completed scan in a recording and lists the others.
- **Mode 06**: a minimum above the maximum means signed 16-bit limits; value, limits and the pass/fail use the signed numbers (`TestResult.signed`).
- **Link and phone**: a failed GATT write is logged with the Android status code and the operation-slot state, and retried once before the link is abandoned; any reply over 2 s is logged (`slow-reply`); partial wake lock while a session runs (4 h cap); the foreground service adds the `location` type when location is granted; a card explains and asks for the battery-optimisation exemption.
- **Ignition off**: every ECU answering NO DATA for 3 s gives `SessionState.VehicleAsleep` ("Vehicle asleep (ignition off)"); polling stops, `01 00` goes out every 10 s, polling resumes when an ECU answers. No adapter re-initialisation meanwhile.
- **Calculated gear** `transmission.gear_calc` (provenance inferred, value 0 = unknown): revs per km/h learned online from steady stretches above 10 km/h (clusters, persisted per vehicle), numbered from the lowest ratio met. Reverse is never reported. Numbering is only right once first gear has been driven steadily; tested on `fixtures/gear/` (one real stop-and-go drive: mostly unknown, a few established ratios, about 33 rpm per km/h dominant).
- No UDS session control (10 03), tester present (3E) or any other new service was added; `ReadOnlyPolicy` is unchanged.

## Drive Truth and the new UI (2026-11-23)

- **Screens** (friend-ready UI, `app/android/.../ui`): guided connect flow with eight steps (`GuidedStep.kt`: adapter, ignition, car answers, car identified, health check, ready to drive, drive detected, report), car summary and garage profile (`SummaryScreen`, `ScanScreen`), faults, live values, the Drive Truth card (`DriveTruthCard.kt`, `DriveTruthCardModel.kt`), findings export (`FindingsExport.kt`, consent in `SettingsScreen.kt`), fuel price card, design system (`KlColors`, `KlSpacing`). "Demo drive" (debug builds) is the canned `DemoDrive.kt` frame list and is unchanged.
- **How a report is made**: the reducer is `core/runtime/.../drivetruth/DriveTruth.kt` (pure, tested). `DriveTruthFeed` (`app/session`) is its live feed: `SessionController.feedReadings` hands every new telemetry snapshot to `DriveTruthFeed.accept`, which turns each new reading (by its own session-clock time, `TimedReading.at`) into `DriveSample`s: `vehicle.speed` (PID 0D), `engine.rpm` (0C), `fuel.rate_lph` (5E, `(256A+B)/20`), `fuel.rate_gps` (9D, `(256A+B)/50`), `intake.maf` (10, `(256A+B)/100`), `fuel.level_pct` (2F), `engine.coolant_temp` (05), `engine.throttle_pct` (11). All were already in `definitions/standard/sae-j1979-mode01.json` with these formulas, so no definition changed. The phone's GPS lines (the same ones the recording gets) are fed too, stamped on the session clock; `engineRunning` is rpm above 400. A drive starts at the first sample with speed above 0 and the engine running, and the report is handed over once when the reducer says it ended (60 s standing with the engine off, or `VehicleAsleep`). `SessionController.publishDriveTruth` then shows it. A refuel (the session's fuel fill count rises) and a fault code that was not there at the drive start (from the fault refresh) become events.
- **Honesty rules** (reducer): every figure is nullable, null is "not available", never zero; fuel comes from 5E, else 9D (density assumed, says so), else air flow for a car the owner says is petrol only (stoichiometric, says so), else the level drop (approximate); diesel and every unknown fuel type never get fuel from air flow; cost needs a price; gaps in polling (passive capture, link loss) are gaps, never filled, and too many gaps drop the figure; GPS distance is a separate number, the two are only compared above 3 km. The price, fuel type and tank are read when the drive starts. The tank is the 50 L of the known car (`TANK_LITRES`).
- **Kept and spoken**: the finished report of each car is kept in the settings store (`DriveTruthStore`, key `drive_truth.<make>-<vin last 4>`, versioned JSON, a damaged entry reads as none) and shown as "last drive" while parked on the next connection. A drive still under way when the user disconnects is kept too (marked "not ended") when it moved for at least a minute. With spoken status on, one line is spoken when a drive ends (`SessionService.announceDrive`); no new setting.
- **Hardening**: the parked body scan can be skipped (`VehicleSession.skipBodyScan`, `BodyOutcome.SKIPPED`, `Limits.skip`; the Skip button of the health check ends whichever parked work runs; the rest of the units is asked in the background as after the time cap). The findings zip is made by `FindingsExport.write`, which refuses (throws, writes nothing) when the report's VIN or its part after the manufacturer code is in any entry; the recording is scrubbed (VIN), GPS lines are dropped, and the dashboard odometer and range lines and events and the tyre sensor lines are left out; the consent text and the README inside the zip list exactly what it holds (a test reads the consent strings). The fuel price field takes only plain positive numbers up to 1000 (digits with one point or comma; no `5f`, `1e2`, `NaN`).
- **Known gaps**: the learning ladder's "Flagged" can only happen parked, before a drive, so it is not an event of the drive; a price typed after the drive does not change that drive's report; hybrid and CNG cars are treated as diesel (no air-flow fuel); the UI's "report ready" can wait if the guided drive stage ends (60 s, rpm 0) while the reducer has not seen the engine off; nothing here has been driven on the real car yet (tests use fake readings).
